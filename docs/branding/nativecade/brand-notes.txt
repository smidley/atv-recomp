Nativecade
Classic games, built for Apple TV

Working name selected on October 28, 2026.

Visual identity
A bold coral N with a mint play triangle. The simple silhouette gives the Apple TV tile a clear focal point from across the room. The wordmark uses friendly, substantial geometric lettering.

Artwork status
These are raster design masters and an icon concept, not an installed Xcode asset catalog. A production tvOS icon needs separate foreground/background layers and exact catalog exports; Apple’s guidance uses an 800 × 480 landscape image stack with parallax. Preserve the emblem’s generous clear space and let the operating system supply the outer mask.

Apple icon guidance: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons

The Nativecade name and art do not imply that game runtime integration is complete. The current launcher and separate Shipwright simulator experiment retain their existing integration status.
